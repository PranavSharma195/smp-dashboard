How scores and data work

Viability score (0–100)

{{ disclaimer }}

Each factor becomes a 0–100 sub-score, then weights combine them:

  • Demand: the stored trend score.
  • Margin: 0 at ≤0%, full marks at 60% gross margin.
  • Investment: full marks at Rs. 5,000 total, zero at Rs. 1,00,000.
  • Competition: low 100, medium 60, high 25.
  • MOQ: full marks at ≤20 units, zero at ≥500.
  • Sourcing complexity: low 100, medium 65, high 30.
  • Confidence: high 100, medium 65, low 30.

Calculations

Landed total = product cost + shipping + insurance/handling + estimated duties & taxes + other. Duties are charged on product + shipping + insurance. Gross profit = selling price − landed cost per unit. Margin = profit ÷ selling price.

Gross profit is not net profit. Advertising, platform fees, storage, returns, delivery and staff costs reduce actual earnings. Duty rates are user-set estimates, not verified customs rates.
